About the Newsletter

As part of an effort to better communicate with our members and provide them with information and resources, the President of SWPA (Lauren Scharff) initiated the creation of quarterly e-newsletters during the summer of 2008. These newsletters contain regional research highlights columns, professional / applied psychology columns, student perspectives columns, past presidents columns, an events calendar, member and conference news, and more! All current members receive email links to new issues. Please share the newsletter link with your colleagues!

Explore past issues using the menu on the left, or jump directly to the current issue above.
