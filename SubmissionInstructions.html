How to Register

  1. Navigate to your profile page using the “Submit an Abstract Here” button.
  2. Create a profile if you do not have one; otherwise, log in.
  3. If you have forgotten your login information, contact us here.
  4. Once logged in, click “Register for SWPA 2026.”
  5. After registration and payment, the “Submit Abstract” button will appear.
  6. Click “Submit Abstract” to begin the submission process.

Important Submission Guidelines

SWPA only accepts abstracts through the SWPA online submission system. Before submitting:

  • Choose the correct profile type.
  • At least one author must be fully registered and paid.
  • All authors must have completed profiles.
  • Each affiliate organization manages its own review process.

After All Authors Have Profiles

  • Log in and click “Submit Abstract.”
  • Select the correct organization.
  • Choose your presentation type.
  • Prepare your abstract according to the instructions.
  • Enter each co-author (only authors with profiles can be added).

SWPA Submission Formats & Types

Attendees may submit abstracts for:

  1. Posters
  2. Talks
  3. Symposia
  4. Workshops
  5. Special Meetings

Posters & Talks

Abstracts must include:

  1. Problem
  2. Method
  3. Results
  4. Conclusions

Abstracts are limited to 500 words. No tables or figures.

Presentation Categories

Empirical Research

  • Reports original research
  • Includes data collection and analysis
  • Applicable to Talks and Posters

Theoretical Contributions

  • Emphasizes theoretical analysis
  • No data collection required
  • Not eligible for student awards

Research Proposals

  • Clarifies background and proposed methodology
  • Not eligible for student awards
  • Exclusive to Posters

Student Competition Submissions

  • Follow competition procedures
  • Submit full 450–500 word abstracts
  • Finalists present talks
  • More info: Student Competitions

Symposia

Symposium submissions must include:

  1. A title (12-word max)
  2. A 50-word symposium abstract
  3. Summaries of each participant’s contribution (combined max 500 words)

All participants must be SWPA members.

Workshops & Special Meetings

SWPA provides rooms for workshops and special meetings.

  • Some workshops may qualify for CE credit
  • Contact Program Chair Dr. Tom Faulkenberry (CONTACT DR. FAULKENBERRY)

Workshop submissions must include:

  1. A title (12-word max)
  2. A workshop abstract (max 500 words)

Teaching Demonstrations

Teaching demonstrations follow the same guidelines as workshops.
